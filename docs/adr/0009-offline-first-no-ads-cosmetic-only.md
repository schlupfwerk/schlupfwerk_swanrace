# 0009. Offline first, no ads, cosmetic-only monetization

- **Status:** Accepted; monetization superseded by [0012](0012-no-paid-items.md)
- **Date:** 2026-11-24

## Context

The game is for the whole family, including children. Trust matters more than
short-term revenue.

## Decision

- The whole game works offline against bots.
- **No ads and no data collection.** The local save (skins, records,
  settings) is never sent anywhere. Online features are optional.
- Monetization: **cosmetic skins** and an optional **tip** only. Nothing that
  gives an advantage in the race. On Steam, the exact model (DLC or in-game
  purchase) is still to be decided.
- No open chat, and no external links without a parental check.

## Consequences

- Simpler privacy declarations in the stores.
- Lower revenue potential, accepted by design.
