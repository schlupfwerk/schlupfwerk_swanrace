# Names and logos

"Schlupfwerk", the Schlupfwerk logo and pixel-egg symbol, and the name of this
game, Swan Race, identify the official game made by
Schlupfwerk. They are not covered by the GNU GPL nor by the asset license.

**You may:**
- mention the names to refer to this project, for example "a fork of Swan
  Race by Schlupfwerk" or "based on Swan Race";
- keep the names and logo in an unmodified copy of this repository and in
  personal builds you do not distribute.

**You may not, without written permission:**
- publish a game, in any store or website, using these names or logos as its
  name, icon or branding;
- suggest that your version is official or endorsed by Schlupfwerk.

If you distribute a modified version, please give it a different name and
icon, and replace the logo files. Questions: contato@schlupfwerk.com
